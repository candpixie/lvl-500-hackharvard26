# ESpaS data

Files in this folder are copied unchanged from ESpaS (Estimator for Space Sustainability),
https://gitlab.com/sustainable-computing-systems/espas, commit 377f1d40 (Jul 13 2026).
ESpaS is dual-licensed MIT / Apache-2.0. Paper: Ohs et al., "Dirty Bits in Low-Earth Orbit:
The Carbon Footprint of Launching Computers", arXiv 2508.06250.

- `*_ds.csv`: design-space sweeps (mission time 1 to 10 years, redundancy 1 to 5).
- `configs/*.toml`: the system definitions those sweeps were generated from.
